LUMEN FOR macOS

Install
  Drag Kickline into the Applications folder.

First launch
  If macOS says Kickline "can't be opened" or is from an unidentified developer:
    1. Try to open Kickline once and dismiss the message.
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Kickline, then confirm.
  You only need to do this once.

Permissions Kickline asks for
  System Audio Recording - to hear rekordbox directly. Say Allow.
  Microphone             - only if you use an audio interface or BlackHole.
  Local Network          - for Art-Net / sACN and Ableton Link. Say Allow.
  If you said Don't Allow by accident, turn them on in
  System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording /
  Microphone / Local Network, then quit and reopen Kickline.

Getting rekordbox audio into Kickline (macOS 14.2 or newer) - recommended
  1. Start rekordbox and play a track.
  2. In Kickline open Audio and click "Use rekordbox (direct, no BlackHole)".
  You keep hearing everything through your controller as normal.

Locking the lights to rekordbox's beat (Ableton Link)
  1. rekordbox must be in Performance mode. Click LINK in the global section,
     and LINK on the decks you play.
  2. In Kickline click "Link" in the top bar. It shows the peer count and BPM.
  3. Use "Beat" and "Bar" as triggers (Next clip on, Audio flash) for flashes
     that land exactly on the beat. "Fire early" (Audio > Timing & sync)
     cancels the delay of your nodes and fixtures; start around 20 ms.

Older macOS, or if you prefer BlackHole
  1. Install BlackHole 2ch (free): https://existential.audio/blackhole/
  2. Open Audio MIDI Setup, click +, "Create Multi-Output Device",
     tick your speakers/controller AND BlackHole 2ch.
  3. In rekordbox Preferences > Audio, set the output to that Multi-Output Device
     (so you still hear it), or to BlackHole directly.
  4. In Kickline open Audio and choose "BlackHole 2ch" as the source.

Art-Net networking
  Plug the Mac into the same switch as your node. Many nodes use 2.x.x.x:
  System Settings > Network > (your Ethernet) > Details > TCP/IP >
  Configure IPv4: Manually, IP 2.0.0.100, Subnet mask 255.0.0.0.
  Then set each lumiverse's Target IP in Advanced Output to the node's IP.

Something not working?
  Open Diagnostics, write what happened in the box, click Export report.
  A file named lumen-report-....json appears on your Desktop. Send that file.

Files
  Projects:       ~/Documents/Kickline
  Crash reports:  ~/Library/Application Support/Kickline/crashes
